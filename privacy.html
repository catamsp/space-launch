At Space Launch, your privacy is our highest priority. We believe a launcher should act as a personal tool to organize your digital life, not as a window into it. This Privacy Policy explains our strict "On-Device First" approach to your data, strictly adhering to Google Play's Developer Program Policies regarding sensitive user data.

1. Information We Access and How We Use It

To provide core launcher functionalities, Space Launch requests access to certain sensitive permissions. None of this data is ever collected, transmitted to our servers, sold, or shared with third parties. All processing occurs strictly locally on your device.

  • Contacts (READ_CONTACTS): We access your contacts solely to allow you to search for and dial contacts directly from the launcher's universal search bar. This data is indexed locally and never leaves your device.
  • Calendar (READ_CALENDAR): We access your calendar events to display your upcoming schedule in the Agenda widget. This data is read temporarily for display purposes and is not stored or transmitted.
  • Notifications (BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE): We require Notification Access to display media playback controls and active app alerts on your home screen widgets. Notification content is processed in real-time on your device and is never logged or uploaded.
  • App Usage (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS): We use usage statistics to power "smart suggestions," displaying your most frequently used apps in the app drawer. This calculation is performed entirely on your device's local storage.
  • Storage & Media (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / Media Permissions): We request storage access to allow you to set custom images from your gallery as your launcher wallpaper or custom app icons.
  • Bluetooth (BLUETOOTH_CONNECT): We use this permission to detect and display your currently connected audio devices within the launcher's quick settings interface.

2. Data Collection and Sharing

We do not collect personal data. Because all core processing happens on your device, we have no databases containing your personal information, contacts, calendar events, or usage habits. Consequently, we do not and cannot sell or share your personal data with third parties.

3. Third-Party Services (Weather)

The only feature in Space Launch that requires an external network request is the Weather Widget (if enabled by the user). To provide weather forecasts, the app may securely ping a third-party weather API. This request only utilizes approximate location data (if granted) or a manually entered city name. No personally identifiable information is sent with these requests.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Because Space Launch does not collect or store your data on external servers, there is no account to delete. You maintain complete control over your data. To permanently delete any local preferences, hidden vault settings, or cached data generated by Space Launch, you simply need to:

  • Clear the app's data in your Android Settings (Settings > Apps > Space Launch > Storage > Clear Data), or
  • Uninstall the Space Launch application from your device.

5. Security

We rely on the robust security mechanisms provided by the Android Operating System. Your locally stored preferences and Hidden Vault configurations are sandboxed within the app's private directory, protected by Android's native application sandboxing and your device's lock screen/biometric security.

6. Children's Privacy

Our application is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take necessary actions.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top.
