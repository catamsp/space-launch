Meet Space Launcher

Built around one idea: your wallpaper, your space

Space Launcher keeps the home screen untouched — a wallpaper you love — and layers what you need on top of it. Time, depth, dynamic tools, and quick actions in one place, with everything else one swipe away.

Wallpaper-first home screen

The wallpaper is the home screen. Top info, depth widgets, and dynamic tools sit directly on it — nothing cluttered on top.

Real depth widgets

Built-in visual processing separates the foreground of your wallpaper so it floats in front of the depth clock and text — true dimensional home screen.

One-swipe hidden space

Swipe up and the wallpaper shrinks away, revealing a hidden space with every page you need — all reachable with one finger.

No data collected

Every feature runs locally on your device. Nothing is collected, transmitted, or sold.

Fully on device

Smart suggestions, mindful pause, depth widgets — all processed locally using built-in visual processing.

The home screen

Four layers, one wallpaper

Everything on the home screen is built from four layers — the top info area, depth widgets, dynamic widgets, and quick launch. Each is fully customizable in font, size, and alignment. Swipe up and the wallpaper yields to the hidden space below — every page, one thumb away.

Top Info Area

The top of your wallpaper keeps the essentials at a glance — time, date, a personal greeting, and a day-progress bar. Below them, real-time status icons show what's actually happening on your phone: live network speed, battery percentage, whether an alarm is set, and when your camera or mic is in use. Each icon appears only according to usage state — shown while the feature is in use, then hidden automatically once idle. Every element can be positioned vertically or horizontally, and the whole area follows one global font typography, so it always fits your wallpaper.

  • Time, date, greeting & day progress in one glance
  • Real-time status icons — live network speed, battery percentage, alarm, camera & mic, and more
  • Vertical & horizontal alignment under one global font typography
  • Readable on any wallpaper — except pure white, where white text would disappear

Depth Widgets

The depth clock and depth text feel alive — built-in visual processing separates the foreground object from your wallpaper and renders it in front of the depth widgets for a true 3D effect. Depth time and depth text each take their own font, and you can grow the set just by adding depth shadows and a parallax effect.

  • Depth time & depth text widgets, each with its own font
  • Built-in visual processing, on-device
  • Add more depth with depth shadows & parallax effect
  • Font & alignment tuned to your wallpaper

Dynamic Widgets

Dynamic widgets bring your tools straight to the home screen — to-do list, music player, speed dial, habit tracker, quick launch, agenda, quick settings, smart suggestions, weather, and notifications. You can also add other apps' widgets as system widgets. Each page holds one widget, swiped horizontally, and every widget stays updated without opening its app.

  • Ten built-in tools — to-do, music player, speed dial, habit tracker, quick launch, agenda, quick settings, smart suggestions, weather & notifications
  • Horizontal swiping, one widget per page
  • Add other apps' widgets as system widgets (a few may not render)
  • Swipe up — dynamic widgets displace and fade, clearing the wallpaper for the hidden space
  • Opacity control on every dynamic widget

Quick Launch & Notifications

Both bottom corners hold your one-tap essentials — pin a contact, a shortcut, or any app to open the camera, make a quick call, or scan and pay in a flash. Right above the navigation bar sits a compact notifications strip showing icons for every notification currently active in the system panel, so you always know what's waiting. Both widgets support vertical alignment and opacity control.

  • Pin contacts, shortcuts & apps to both corners
  • Quick call, quick camera, scan & pay
  • Notification icons from the active system panel, placed above the navigation bar
  • Vertical alignment & opacity control on both
