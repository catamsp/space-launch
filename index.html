Core Features & Widgets

Space Launch transforms your Android experience by replacing cluttered grids with purposeful workspaces. Every widget is designed with extreme attention to detail to keep you productive.

Smart Suggestions Widget

Leverages local on-device usage stats to predict and surface the apps you are most likely to need right now, reducing the time spent searching your app drawer.

Weather Widget

Provides a clean, minimalist view of your local weather conditions and forecast without requiring you to open a dedicated weather application.

To-Do Widget

A simple, highly accessible task list right on your home screen. Quickly add, check off, and manage your daily tasks with zero friction.

Agenda Widget

Integrates directly with your device's calendar to show your upcoming events and meetings at a glance, ensuring you never miss an appointment.

Habit Widget

Track your daily goals and habits visually. A persistent reminder on your home screen helps build consistency and mindfulness.

Music & Video Widgets

Control your active media playback directly from the launcher. Play, pause, skip, and view current track information instantly.

Speed Dial & Quick Launch Widgets

Pin your most important contacts for one-tap calling, and set up quick shortcuts to your most frequently used actions or apps.

Quick Settings & Notifications Widgets

Access essential device toggles (Wi-Fi, Bluetooth) and view your recent notifications in a unified, distraction-free interface.

Comprehensive Settings & Customization

Tailor every aspect of Space Launch to fit your exact workflow and aesthetic preferences. Each setting is designed to give you precise control.

Global Appearance & Themes

  • Icon Pack: Apply custom third-party icon packs to unify your app drawer's look and match your chosen aesthetic.
  • Global Font Family: Choose the primary typeface used throughout the launcher to match your personal style.
  • Depth Time Font & Depth Text Font: Specifically configure the typography used for the lock screen/home screen clock and prominent text elements for a customized depth effect.

Wallpaper & Background Customization

  • Wallpaper: Choose a custom image from your gallery to serve as your launcher's background.
  • Wallpaper Color: Extract or manually set a dominant color from your wallpaper to theme the launcher's UI elements.
  • Wallpaper Dimming: Darken the wallpaper slightly to increase the contrast and legibility of text and icons on your home screen.
  • Wallpaper Gradient: Apply a subtle gradient overlay to your wallpaper for a more dynamic, modern look.
  • Wallpaper Dual Shade: Split the wallpaper background into two complementary shades for a unique, structured aesthetic.
  • Wallpaper Shadow Color: Adjust the color of the shadow cast by text and elements against the wallpaper for perfect visibility.

Card & Layout

  • Bottom Card Layout: Toggle between a distinct floating card style or a standard edge-to-edge layout for the bottom workspaces.
  • Card Color: Customize the background color of the workspace cards to match or contrast with your wallpaper.
  • Card Shadow Color & Emboss: Fine-tune the 3D appearance of cards by adjusting their shadow color and emboss depth for a tactile feel.

App Drawer & Focus Features

  • App Drawer Grid: Toggle the layout of the app drawer between a structured grid and a minimalist list view.
  • Hide App Names: Remove text labels from apps in the drawer for a cleaner, icon-only aesthetic.
  • Show Work Profile Apps: Seamlessly integrate or hide your enterprise/work profile applications within the main drawer.
  • Close Hidden Space on Launch: Automatically lock and close your secure hidden app vault as soon as you launch an app from it.
  • Mindful Pause Apps: Select specific addictive or distracting apps that will trigger a mindful breathing pause before they are allowed to open.
  • Mindful Pause Free Time: Set a daily allowance of free time for your mindful apps before the pause intervention kicks in.

System Integration

  • Swipe down for notifications: Enable a gesture to pull down the system notification shade by swiping anywhere on the home screen.
  • Audio Visualizer: Display a dynamic audio visualizer on the home screen when media is playing (requires notification access).

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 2026

1. Introduction

At Space Launch, your privacy is our highest priority. We believe a launcher should organize your digital life, not monitor it. This policy explains our strict "On-Device First" approach to your data.

2. Data Collection & Usage

We do not collect, transmit, or sell your personal data. All processing happens locally on your device:

  • Contacts (READ_CONTACTS): Used solely to allow you to search and dial contacts from the launcher search bar. Contact data never leaves your device.
  • Calendar (READ_CALENDAR): Used to render the Agenda widget. Event data is processed locally and discarded from memory when not in use.
  • Notifications (NOTIFICATION_LISTENER): Used to display media controls and active alerts. Content is read locally and never logged or transmitted.
  • App Usage (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS): Used locally to calculate your most-used apps for smart suggestions.

3. Third-Party Services

The only external network requests made by Space Launch are for fetching real-time Weather data (if the Weather widget is enabled). This request is anonymized and only utilizes your approximate location.

4. Data Security

Because your sensitive data never leaves your device, it remains protected by your device's native OS security, encryption, and biometric safeguards (such as the Hidden Vault feature).

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our support channels.
